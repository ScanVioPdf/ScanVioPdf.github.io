Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-20 · Last updated: 2026-07-20

This Privacy Policy describes how Foglio ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Foglio mobile application (the "App"). This policy is written to comply with the Google Play User Data policy, Data safety section, and applicable laws including the EU GDPR and the California CCPA/CPRA.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Summary

Foglio is designed to be offline-first. All documents you scan, convert, or edit are processed locally on your device. We do not upload, transmit, or store your documents on any server.

The only categories of data that leave your device are:

  • Advertising identifiers and related device data, collected by Google AdMob to serve ads (if you use the free version of the App).
  • Purchase transaction data, handled by Google Play Billing when you subscribe or make an in-app purchase.
  • Crash and performance diagnostics, if you have not opted out of Google Play's automatic crash reporting.

Foglio itself does not operate any analytics, telemetry, or user-tracking infrastructure.

2.2 Data collected by third-party services

Google AdMob — if you use the free version of the App, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID (AAID)
  • Coarse device information (model, OS version, language, general location derived from IP)
  • Ad interaction events (impressions, clicks)
  • Approximate IP-based location

AdMob's use of this data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Play Services (Billing) — when you make a purchase, Google Play processes your Google account identifier, purchase token, and payment method. We receive only a purchase token used to unlock features. We do not receive your payment card, address, or Google account email.

Google Play (Crash reporting) — Google Play automatically collects crash stack traces and ANR (Application Not Responding) reports for apps installed from the Play Store. You can opt out via your device settings (Settings → Google → Usage & diagnostics).

Google ML Kit — text recognition (OCR) runs entirely on-device. Some language recognizers may download model files from Google servers on first use. No document contents are transmitted.

2.3 Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect, and Foglio has no code that transmits:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials
  • Contents of scanned documents, converted files, or OCR output
  • Contacts, calendar, SMS, or call logs
  • Precise GPS location
  • Biometric data
  • Health, financial, or government-ID data
  • Browsing history outside the App

4. How We Use Information

We do not "use" your document data because we never receive it. Data collected by AdMob is used to select and measure advertising. Data collected by Google Play Billing is used to fulfill your purchase.

Specifically:

  • To operate the App: locally, without server calls.
  • To serve advertising: through AdMob (free tier only).
  • To process purchases: through Google Play Billing.
  • To improve stability: aggregate crash reports from Google Play.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with data brokers.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Documents and other data on your device are retained until you delete them within the App or uninstall the App. Uninstalling removes all local data.

AdMob data is retained by Google according to Google's retention policy.

Purchase records are retained by Google Play for legal and accounting reasons.

Deletion requests: because we do not store personal data on our servers, we cannot delete anything on our side. To delete all data associated with Foglio, uninstall the App. To request deletion of AdMob-collected data, use Google's My Ad Center.

7. Children's Privacy

Foglio is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will investigate.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data processed about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Delete your data (uninstalling the App achieves this for locally-stored data).
  • Object to or restrict processing.
  • Withdraw consent for personalized ads (adjust in your device's Ads settings).
  • Data portability — export your documents from the App at any time using the built-in Share function.

To exercise these rights against Google (as a joint controller for advertising data), see Google's Privacy & Terms. To exercise any right against us, email the address in Section 12.

California residents (CCPA/CPRA): You have the right to know, to delete, to correct, and to opt out of "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising. Foglio does not sell personal information.

EU/UK residents (GDPR/UK GDPR): The legal bases we rely on are consent (advertising) and contract (paid features). You may lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. Security

Documents are stored in your device's app-private storage, which is sandboxed by the operating system. Encrypted PDFs use industry-standard AES-256 (Android via PDFBox) or AES-128 (iOS via PDFKit).

We do not transmit your documents, so there is no transport-layer risk from Foglio. Third-party SDKs (AdMob, Google Play Billing) use TLS 1.2+ for their own network calls.

No security system is perfect. If you become aware of a vulnerability, report it to the address in Section 12.

10. International Data Transfers

We do not transfer your data internationally because we do not receive it. Google (AdMob, Play Billing) may transfer data to the United States and other countries where its infrastructure operates, under the safeguards described in Google's Privacy Policy.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top and, for material changes, present a notice inside the App on next launch. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
